# Drawing sound

`assets/audio/sfx/pencil-draw.ogg` (and its `.mp3` copy) is a processed excerpt of **Pencil #1 (0221)** from BigSoundBank:
https://bigsoundbank.com/pencil-s0221.html

Source audio: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/0221.mp3
License: CC0 (public domain), verified on the source page on 2026-09-16.

Processing: excerpt from 1.1 to 3.5 seconds, mono 44.1 kHz;
180 Hz high-pass and 6.2 kHz low-pass filters; level normalization with a peak
ceiling, 35 ms fade-in, and 80 ms fade-out; then encoded to Ogg Opus and MP3. The game applies a gentle volume
envelope and a small playback-speed variation synchronized to the drawing.
The recording is bundled locally; playback does not require an external service.
